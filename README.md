# Airline-Passenger-Satisfaction-Analysis

## 📌 Overview:
An Exploratory Data Analysis project focused on understanding passenger satisfaction using airline passenger survey data. The analysis covers travel class, customer type, service ratings, flight distance and delays.

## 🛠️ Libraries Used:
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## 🔍 Key Insights:
- Business class has the most passengers, and about 70% of them are satisfied.
- Only 43.9% of all passengers are satisfied, and 56.1% are neutral or dissatisfied.
- Personal travellers are much less satisfied (10%) than business travellers (59%).
- Satisfaction is highest for passengers aged 41-60.
- Inflight wifi and online booking are the lowest rated services.
- Online boarding has the strongest link with satisfaction.
- Departure delay and arrival delay are very strongly related.
